numerology reading - no account, no data leaving your phone.

Your name and birth date never leave your device. There's no sign-up,
no ads, and no third-party tracking. Read the full privacy policy at
the link in this listing.

Choose Pythagorean or Chaldean numerology, and pick a light or dark
look - or let AstroMatrix follow your system setting.
