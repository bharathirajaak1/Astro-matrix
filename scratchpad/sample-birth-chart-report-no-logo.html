Understanding Your Numerology Blueprint

Pythagorean Numerology

Your Core Numbers (Life Path, Destiny, Soul Urge, Personality, and Birthday) come from Pythagorean numerology - a traditional approach that assigns a number to each letter of your name and reduces your birth date to find a small set of core numbers, then looks at what each number has traditionally been associated with.

The Lo Shu Grid

Your Lo Shu Grid is a separate traditional framework. It places every digit of your full birth date onto a fixed 3x3 grid and looks at which numbers are present, repeated, or missing - a different lens from your Core Numbers, not a recalculation of them.

Bringing Them Together

AstroMatrix brings these two traditions together as complementary perspectives on the same birth details, rather than treating either one as the complete picture. Your six Planes of Expression take this further, grouping specific Lo Shu Grid positions into six thematic readings of the same grid.

Your Five Core Numbers

Life Path 4 is traditionally associated with practicality, stability and building things step by step. You may prefer having a clear plan and knowing what needs to be done before you begin. You may also take your responsibilities seriously and value things that are reliable and lasting.

Destiny - sometimes called Expression - is traditionally associated with the abilities and potential a person may naturally be able to express. It is calculated from every letter of your full birth name.

Destiny 1 is traditionally associated with expressing self-direction and original thinking through your abilities. This can point toward a working style where you prefer finding your own solution rather than following an existing one, and where your contribution often takes the form of starting something rather than maintaining it. People with this number are often described as able to turn an idea into self-directed action.

Natural Strengths
